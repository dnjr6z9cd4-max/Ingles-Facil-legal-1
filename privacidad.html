1. DATA CONTROLLER

Fonsek Companies LLC (Holland, Michigan, USA) is the data controller responsible for your personal data. For any privacy question or request, contact: hola.inglesfacil@gmail.com.

2. DATA WE COLLECT

  • Account data: email address, display name, profile photo URL (if provided by Sign in with Apple or Google), and an authentication identifier (UID). Source: Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, or email/password.
  • Learning data: selected level (A1–C1), lesson progress, XP, streak, last-login timestamp, messages exchanged with Buddy (text), Situation scenes practiced and answers.
  • Voice / microphone: short audio captured by the device microphone is processed on-device by the operating system's speech-recognition engine (Apple Speech / Android SpeechRecognizer). Raw audio is never uploaded to our servers. Only the resulting transcribed text is sent to the AI tutor.
  • Subscription & entitlement data: subscription status (active/trial/expired), product identifier, anonymous RevenueCat App User ID, platform (iOS/Android), app version.
  • Device & technical data: device model, operating system version, app version, language, time zone, crash logs and basic diagnostics (via Firebase).
  • Usage analytics: session timestamps, features used, daily message count to Buddy (for fair-use limits and refund validation). We do not collect precise GPS location, contacts, photos, calendar, SMS, or browsing history.
  • Local storage: the App stores non-sensitive preferences (e.g., last-screen, audio-volume setting) in local device storage. No cross-site tracking cookies are used.

5. AI PROCESSING (BUDDY)

Buddy is powered by Google Gemini (Generative Language API) for conversation and content generation, and by Google Cloud Text-to-Speech for voice synthesis. When you chat with Buddy, the text of your message and a short conversation history are sent to Google's API solely for the purpose of generating an educational response. Under the paid Generative Language API tier we use, Google does not use your prompts or responses to train its general AI models. AI output may be inaccurate; do not rely on Buddy for legal, medical, immigration, or financial advice.
